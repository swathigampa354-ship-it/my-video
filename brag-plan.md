# Brag Plan: TryPost

## What is this app?
TryPost (trypost.it) is an agentic social media scheduler: you connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any MCP client, and the agent plans, writes, and schedules a week of posts across 13 networks from one drag-and-drop calendar — $19/mo flat, unlimited accounts and team members.

## The angle
The scheduler that schedules itself. While other tools sell you another dashboard, TryPost's demo asks an AI agent in plain English — "Plan this weekend for my coffee shop." — and watches it fill a real content calendar (Fri promo, Sat behind-the-scenes, Sun treat) and then report last week's numbers. The video recreates that exact working-app moment: prompt → agent works → calendar fills → analytics report. The joke is how casually it replaces five open tabs; the flex is that every line on screen is the product's own copy.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dark screen, a chat input, and a prompt typing itself out character by character with keyboard ticks: "Plan this weekend for my coffee shop." Below it, small: "Connect ChatGPT, Claude, or any MCP client". You know in one second this video is about telling an agent what to do — not about a dashboard.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The agent reply bubble: "Writing captions and scheduling posts…" typing dots → then the success chip slams in verbatim: "Scheduled 3 posts to Instagram, TikTok, and X" with a check icon.
- The content calendar card (warm paper, black border, violet accent) fills row by row: FRI "Happy hour: 2-for-1 coffees this Friday", SAT "Behind the scenes: roasting Saturday's beans", SUN "Weekend treat: free cookie with any latte" — plus "Publishing to" network chips (Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, +8 more).
- The analytics ask: "How did last week's posts do?" → three stat cards pop with count-ups: Reach 24.5K (+18%), Engagement 6.1% (+2.3pt), New followers 312 (+9%), and "Best day Saturday · 11:30".

## Outro / punchline
Cut back to dark. The site's own final CTA as the punchline: "Stop posting to five apps by hand." — then the brand chip lands: TryPost · $19/mo · unlimited accounts & seats · trypost.it.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, straight from the product's live demo:
1. Type a plain-English request into the agent chat.
2. Agent writes captions and schedules posts.
3. Calendar fills (3 scheduled) and posts publish to every network; agent then reports last week's analytics.
This is the centerpiece: scenes 1–4 are this flow, recreated with the product's own data shapes (fictional coffee-shop posts that appear on the site's demo).

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: "agentic launch film — one prompt, a whole week of posts; the product talks, the calendar fills, the numbers report"
- Interpretation: Playful, clean, postable. Fast motion and quick transitions, but every readable line holds. The humor comes from how matter-of-factly the agent does your job — no winking, no hype language.

- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; RMS/bass modulates the violet glow halo behind the chat card (scenes 1-2) and the calendar card's soft violet shadow (scene 3). No waveform/equalizer visuals, no pulsing text.
- SFX posture: moderate; motion-matched; low-HF-risk picks (impact family preferred; casino card-place for calendar rows at 0.55-0.75 volume)
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Scene 1 prompt typing — randomized keyboard/keypress-*.wav per character

Sequential/interaction: yes — per-character typing with randomized keypress ticks; send button pulses violet once typing ends.

Audio-coupled idea: keyboard keypress wavs per character; a soft click on the send pulse at ~2.9s.
